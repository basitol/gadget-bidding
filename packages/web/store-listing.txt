Full description:
GadgetBid is a Nigerian marketplace for phones, laptops, tablets, and other gadgets.

Buyers
- Browse live English auctions priced in Naira
- Place bids with a ₦1,000 wallet commitment hold
- Pay winning bids through your GadgetBid wallet, funded with Paystack
- Confirm delivery after the item ships from backoffice

Sellers
- List gadgets for review before they go live
- Track bids and orders in the app
- Receive payouts after delivery confirmation, minus a 5% platform fee

Payments
Wallet funding and order payments use Paystack. GadgetBid does not store card numbers.
