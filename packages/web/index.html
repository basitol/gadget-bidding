Nigerian gadget auctions

GadgetBid is a marketplace for phones, laptops, and other gadgets. Buyers bid in timed English auctions. Payments go through a wallet funded with Paystack. Winning bids sit in escrow until delivery.

Buyers Browse live auctions, place bids with a ₦1,000 commitment hold, and pay within 24 hours if you win.
Sellers List a gadget, pass review, and receive payout after the buyer confirms delivery. Sales include a 5% platform fee.
How to use GadgetBid The marketplace is available in the GadgetBid mobile app on Google Play. This site hosts legal pages and support contacts.
